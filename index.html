We love all events

(especially weddings)

  • Coordination

    You plan it, and we step in 8 weeks before to ensure a successful and smooth event by taking over vendor correspondence, building a timeline, and being the point person for your vendor team. We’re here to advocate for you and give any emotional support needed from the moment you sign with us, but most importantly the day of your event

  • Partial Planning

    We’ll be your guides and a helpful resource throughout your planning process to make sure nothing slips through the cracks. While you do most of the planning yourself, you’ll feel good knowing you have our support while keeping you on track.

  • Full Planning

    Whether or not you’ve booked your venue or know your date, we will work with you from the very beginning. We will get to know you and help to build a spectacular event that will look like no other, because it’s yours!
